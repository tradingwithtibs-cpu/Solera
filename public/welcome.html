    How it works

    Three steps. The third one is real money, and it says so.

    Most trading apps start you at the checkout. Solera starts you at the whiteboard: look around, watch how the wallets that are already in behave, and move to live when you are ready.

    1. Step one

      Practice at live prices

      No wallet needed. Buy a slice of NVDAx with practice cash, at the same Solana price a real order would get. Write why you bought it and what would prove you wrong. Solera scores that thesis against what the price does next.

      Practice mode
    2. Step two

      Follow the wallets that hold it

      The leaderboard and every “held by” list are the largest real wallets holding each token, read from the chain and linked to Solscan. Follow one, copy a holding, or just watch how the patient ones behave.

      Leaderboard · Discover
    3. Step three

      Go live from your own wallet

      Connect Phantom or Solflare and the same ticket builds a Jupiter Ultra order you sign yourself, with the real route, real price and real fees shown first. Solera never holds your keys or your funds.

      Live · mainnet
    Product

    It reads the market for you. It never tells you what to do.

    Everything on screen is a reading: what a token costs, who holds it, what people are saying, where the gaps are. Nothing is a recommendation, and nothing is seeded to look busier than it is.

    Every xStock on Solana, priced live.

    • 737 xStocks, priced in batches through Jupiter

    Discover

    A feed people shape, not an algorithm.

    Headlines and real fills with their notes, ranked by real votes with comments underneath. Scores start at zero. Trending is what the largest on-chain wallets actually hold this week, not what is being promoted.

    • Upvotes and downvotes, comments on every post
    • Trending by value held across top wallets
    • Ask the agent about any story, right from the row

    Plans and the agent

    Say it in a sentence. Arm it with a tap.

    “If TSLAx falls to $300, sell 5 shares.” Solera shows you the rule it understood and arms it only when you tap. Practice plans fill on the server about once a minute. Live plans become Jupiter Trigger orders you sign once. The agent reads prices, news and your plans, proposes cards with buttons, and signs nothing.

    • A plain-English parser that answers even without a model
    • Claude behind it when a key is present, with strict tools
    • Every proposal is a card; nothing is armed by the model
    Available on your mobile device

    Solera shows you what the market is doing and what other people are doing. It never tells you what to do.
